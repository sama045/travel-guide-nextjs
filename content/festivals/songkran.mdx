The **Songkran Festival** is Thailand's traditional New Year celebration, held every year from **13–15 April**. Known worldwide as the country's famous water festival, Songkran combines ancient Buddhist traditions with lively street celebrations, creating one of the most exciting cultural events in Southeast Asia.

The festival begins with meaningful customs such as visiting temples, making merit, pouring scented water over Buddha images, and respectfully blessing elders by gently pouring water over their hands. These rituals symbolize purification, gratitude, and good fortune for the coming year.

Across Thailand, the atmosphere transforms into a nationwide celebration. Streets become lively with water fights, music, parades, and local performances. Popular destinations like Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Pattaya, and Phuket attract millions of visitors who join locals in celebrating this unique tradition.

Whether you want to experience authentic Thai culture or simply enjoy one of the world's biggest water festivals, Songkran offers unforgettable memories for travelers of all ages.
