The **Vegetarian Festival** is a nine-day religious celebration observed by Thai-Chinese communities throughout Thailand, especially in **Phuket**, during the **ninth lunar month**, usually in **September or October**.

Participants follow a strict vegetarian diet while observing spiritual practices believed to purify the body and mind. The festival also features temple ceremonies, dragon dances, firecrackers, and colorful street processions.

One of the festival's most famous traditions is the extraordinary acts of devotion performed by spirit mediums, who participate in ritual body piercings as a demonstration of faith and protection from harm.

In addition to its spiritual significance, the festival offers visitors the chance to sample a wide variety of delicious vegetarian dishes prepared by local vendors and restaurants throughout the celebration.
