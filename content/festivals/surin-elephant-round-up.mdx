The **Surin Elephant Round-Up** is one of Thailand's most spectacular cultural events, held annually in **Surin Province** during **November**.

The festival celebrates the long-standing relationship between the local Kui people and elephants. Visitors can watch hundreds of elephants participate in impressive demonstrations, including traditional processions, historical battle reenactments, elephant football, and various skill performances.

Beyond the main shows, guests can explore local markets, cultural exhibitions, and traditional Isaan performances that highlight the region's rich heritage.

The Surin Elephant Round-Up is an extraordinary opportunity to learn about Thailand's elephant culture while experiencing one of the country's most iconic festivals.
