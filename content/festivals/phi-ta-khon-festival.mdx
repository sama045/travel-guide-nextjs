The **Phi Ta Khon Festival**, also known as the Ghost Festival, is a unique cultural celebration held in **Dan Sai District, Loei Province**, typically during **June or July**.

The festival is famous for participants wearing colorful handmade ghost masks, elaborate costumes, and dancing through the streets in lively parades. The event is believed to originate from a Buddhist legend in which spirits joined the joyful celebration of Prince Vessantara's return.

Visitors can enjoy traditional music, cultural performances, local food, and religious ceremonies throughout the multi-day event. While the costumes may appear playful or spooky, the festival represents good fortune, community spirit, and Buddhist beliefs.

Phi Ta Khon offers an unforgettable cultural experience that blends mythology, religion, and local traditions unlike anywhere else in Thailand.
