The **Loy Krathong Festival** is one of Thailand's most beautiful and enchanting celebrations, taking place on the full moon night of the **12th lunar month**, usually in **November**.

The word "Loy" means "to float," while "Krathong" refers to a small decorative basket made from banana leaves, flowers, candles, and incense. During the festival, people release their krathongs onto rivers, lakes, and canals to express gratitude to the Goddess of Water and let go of bad luck and negative energy.

Many regions celebrate with cultural performances, fireworks, beauty contests, and traditional music. In northern Thailand, particularly Chiang Mai, the festival is often celebrated alongside **Yi Peng**, where thousands of glowing sky lanterns are released into the night sky, creating a breathtaking spectacle.

Loy Krathong is a peaceful and romantic celebration that beautifully reflects Thailand's spiritual traditions and appreciation for nature.
