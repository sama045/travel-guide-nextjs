The **Candle Festival** is an important Buddhist celebration held annually in **Ubon Ratchathani** during **July**, marking the beginning of **Buddhist Lent (Khao Phansa)**.

The festival originated from the tradition of offering candles to temples, providing monks with light during the rainy season retreat. Over time, this simple custom evolved into a spectacular event featuring enormous wax sculptures carved with remarkable artistic detail.

Visitors can enjoy grand candle parades, traditional dance performances, music, cultural exhibitions, and local handicraft markets. Skilled artisans spend months creating intricate wax masterpieces depicting Buddhist stories, mythology, and Thai heritage.

The Candle Festival is a perfect opportunity to experience Thailand's artistic excellence, religious traditions, and warm local hospitality in one unforgettable celebration.
